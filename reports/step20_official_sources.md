## IEEE JBHI
- Home: https://www.embs.org/jbhi/
- Author requirements: https://www.embs.org/jbhi/for-authors/
- Prepare/submit, page limit, APC, review model, public-data and IRB language: https://www.embs.org/jbhi/prepare-and-submit-your-manuscript/
- FAQ: https://www.embs.org/jbhi/faqs/
- Official template selector: https://template-selector.ieee.org/secure/templateSelector/publicationType

Verified requirements used: regular papers; IEEE single-spaced double-column format; embedded figures/tables; abstract <=250 words; numeric square-bracket references; 14-page regular-paper limit including supplementary material; hybrid Traditional/OA model; 2026 OA APC US$2,800; anonymous peer review with at least three reviewers for regular papers; public database source/reference and explicit IRB reference requirement for applicable human-subject studies.

## IEEE TBME
- Home: https://www.embs.org/tbme/
- Prepare a manuscript: https://www.embs.org/tbme/prepare-a-manuscript/
- FAQ: https://www.embs.org/tbme/faq/
- Submission portal: https://ieee.atyponrex.com/journal/tbme-embs

Verified requirements used: IEEE double-column format; embedded figures/tables; structured abstract under 250 words with Objective, Methods, Results, Conclusion, and Significance; conclusion <=300 words; one review file under 10 MB; cover letter under 250 words; confidence intervals encouraged and p-values requested for comparisons.

## IEEE OJEMB
- Home: https://www.embs.org/ojemb/
- Instructions: https://www.embs.org/ojemb/manuscript-formats/
- Official templates: https://www.embs.org/ojemb/ieee-ojemb-templates/
- Submission portal: https://mc.manuscriptcentral.com/oj-emb

Verified requirements used: science/technology papers have 14-page limits; about 3,000 words of text plus about 4,000 words of supplementary material and up to 10 figures/tables; visual summary first page; impact statement; defined section order.
