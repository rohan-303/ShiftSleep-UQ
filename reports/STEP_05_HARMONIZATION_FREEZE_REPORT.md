## 5. ISRUC Final Audit

* official source: `https://sleeptight.isr.uc.pt/`; download page `https://sleeptight.isr.uc.pt/?page_id=48`
* NEMAR relationship: `https://data.nemar.org/nm000111/v1.0.1/` is a public BIDS-formatted derivative generated by MNE-BIDS 0.17.0, with source URL pointing to ISRUC. It is not silently treated as byte-identical original raw provenance. It reports 118 participants and 126 scans.
* cohorts: ISRUC-S1 = 100 participants/recordings, sleep-related disorders; ISRUC-S2 = 8 participants with 2 recordings each (16 recordings), repeated-session healthy cohort; ISRUC-S3 = 10 healthy participants/recordings.
* participants: 118 total according to NEMAR derivative; official cohort sum is 118.
* recordings: 126 total (100 + 16 + 10); sessions are one per S1/S3 participant and two per S2 participant.
* channels: official page names EEG, EOG, EMG, ECG, respiratory and SpO2 families. NEMAR representative channel sidecars list 19/20 signals but type them as EEG and provide no reliable EOG type/count; exact primary selection is therefore not frozen.
* rates: official/derivative representative metadata reports 200 Hz; representative EDF range attempts were made for S1, S2 sessions 1/2, and S3. Sidecars report 200 Hz, but semantic channel typing remains insufficient.
* scorers: official site says two human experts; NEMAR events preserve scorer-1 labels with scorer-2 values in extras for inspected records. A primary original-stream convention is not frozen until original annotation files are directly verified.
* annotation semantics: NEMAR README reports 30-second events and codes W=0, N1=1, N2=2, N3=3, R=5, U=6; original source documentation and scorer representation remain required for primary use.
* subject identity: NEMAR `sub-I###`, `sub-II###/ses-1`, `ses-2`, and `sub-III###` provide deterministic participant/session identity. All S2 sessions remain grouped.
* access state: PUBLIC_METADATA_VERIFIED; original full archive is not downloaded in Step 5. ISRUC remains deferred for preprocessing.

## 18. Synthetic Missingness Contract

A missing modality must exist in the raw recording and be intentionally withheld at condition time. The future input representation must carry an explicit modality-availability mask, and the absent branch/input must not be confusable with physiological zero. Allowed primary conditions are all-present, no-EEG, and no-EOG.

## 19. Structural Mismatch Contract
