\section*{S1. Dataset and channel details}
The benchmark uses Sleep-EDF Expanded Sleep Cassette recordings and ISRUC Cohort I. The frozen accounting is 78 Sleep-EDF subjects (153 recordings; 414,961 valid epochs) and 100 ISRUC subjects, of which 99 are included after excluding one unsupported layout (89,312 valid epochs). The input channels are EEG Fpz-Cz and horizontal EOG for Sleep-EDF. ISRUC montage families are ISRUC\_A1A2 and ISRUC\_M1M2. Labels are harmonized to Wake, N1, N2, N3, and REM; R\&K stages 3 and 4 map to N3. Movement, unknown, artifact, and unscored epochs are excluded.

\section*{S2. Architecture and training}
B0 is a dual raw-signal branch convolutional model with independent EEG and EOG branches, GroupNorm, GELU, residual blocks, adaptive pooling, 128-dimensional branch embeddings, modality masking, concatenation, and classification (654,597 parameters). B1 uses the same architecture and training protocol, exposing source TRAIN to full input with probability 0.50, EEG-only input with probability 0.25, and EOG-only input with probability 0.25. The frozen methods map is \texttt{reports/step17\_methods\_evidence\_map.md}; checkpoint and configuration manifests remain in the repository evidence package.

\section*{Frozen non-claims}
The study does not establish universal uncertainty failure, universal modality-dropout superiority, montage causality, clinical safety, arbitrary-shift conformal validity, target-free recovery from all shifts, a novel calibration method, state-of-the-art sleep staging, SHHS validation, a new architecture, or performance on unevaluated populations.
