Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, “ShiftSleep-UQ: Calibration and Selective Reliability of Multimodal Sleep Staging Under Compound Dataset and Missing-Modality Shift,” as a regular paper for IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics.

The manuscript addresses a biomedical-health-informatics problem that is often hidden by in-domain, full-modality evaluation: how predictive and reliability behavior changes when sleep-staging systems face reciprocal dataset shift and missing channels. We provide a frozen two-dataset benchmark, target-free primary evaluation, subject-level uncertainty and selective-prediction analyses, conformal transfer diagnostics, and a controlled source-only modality-dropout comparison. The contribution is an evidence-bounded benchmark and reliability study, not a claim of a new architecture or universal uncertainty method.

The primary results show improved compound-shift macro-F1 in three of four prespecified cells, while calibration, error ranking, and conformal behavior remain heterogeneous. The manuscript preserves the inconclusive fourth cell, distinguishes deployable evidence from target-label oracle diagnostics, and provides a reproducible artifact and audit package. These properties align with J-BHI’s focus on biomedical and health informatics at the intersection of information technology and biomedicine.

This manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and contains no invented clinical or prospective-validation claim. Author, funding, ethics, and competing-interest declarations will be completed before submission.

Sincerely,
Anonymous Authors
