# Reviewer 1 — Scientific novelty and contribution

## Summary
This is a controlled benchmark/reliability study rather than a new-model paper. Its strongest contribution is the explicit reciprocal C0–C5 design and separation of predictive and reliability axes.

## Strengths
- Frozen protocol and provenance make the B0/B1 contrast interpretable.
- Reciprocal transfer exposes direction asymmetry.
- The negative D2 C5 result and method non-authorization are retained.

## Weaknesses / major concerns
- Two core datasets and a deliberately simple epoch-wise model limit generality.
- The closest-prior-work boundary must remain modest; no priority claim is warranted.
- The paper must show the full B0 landscape, not only a categorical statement.

## Required revisions
Substantive Table 2, explicit limitations, dataset citations, and clear separation of benchmark contribution from method novelty. These were addressed.

## Minor concerns
Venue-specific comparison and author metadata remain future work.

## Likely score before revision
Borderline / weak accept depending on venue fit; this is a review heuristic, not a scientific result.
